First-level bid screening

Screen bidder submissions against a Tender or RFP — with evidence you can trace.

Built for procurement teams who need a structured first pass: requirements, findings, status, reasons, and document-page evidence. This is a decision-support tool, not a final award or eligibility decision.

Start screening

What you get

Preliminary Compliance Score, requirement-by-requirement results, missing-document alerts, and a risk summary — each tied to document and page references when evidence exists.

Human review remains required before any procurement decision.

How it works

1. Attach documents

Upload one Tender/RFP PDF and one or more bidder PDFs. Optional business registration details can be added for context.

2. Run first-level screening

The future Python pipeline will extract, identify requirements, find evidence, compare, classify, explain, and assess risk.

3. Review traceable results

Follow Requirement → Finding → Status → Reason → Evidence → Document/Page. Download a screening report for the file.

Upload workspace

Files stay in this browser session so a FastAPI backend can receive them later. Stage 1 does not analyze PDF content. Sample results will be shown after you verify.
